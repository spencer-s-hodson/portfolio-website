Hiring managers and recruiters decide whether to interview; freelance clients are secondary. Job: identify Spencer, scan proof, act.

## Content

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one quiet index folio on a soft dark stage — identity is the cover, work is indexed plates — refusing the standard centered-hero-plus-equal-card-grid developer portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal stage `#121212` wraps one warm paper sheet `#f4f2ee`; orange `#ff5a1f` marks the active index only; hairline rules and course bands structure content; Bricolage Grotesque for display, Geist for body; floating pill nav; no competing card shells.

STORY: Visitor meets Spencer and the AI-native role immediately, scans an index of Featured / Experience / Projects, pulls one face-out project, then decides to make contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark stage full-bleed; centered floating pill nav (Home · Projects · Experience); one large rounded paper sheet; cover type Spencer Hodson dominating, role under it, one supporting line; numbered index rows with orange active mark; primary action Get in touch as a stamped sheet action; featured project plate begins at the fold.

FORM: Quiet Index Folio (grounded #4 on re-roll 1); seed key `a0073b81`. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing an index row brightens its course and the face-out plate crossfades to that entry; shared motion clock, reduced-motion holds still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
