# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and recruiters deciding whether to interview the owner for a full-time software engineering role.

Secondary: prospective clients deciding whether to hire the owner for freelance or contract engineering work. Full-time hiring comes first.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio of the owner's own software engineering work. Success is a visitor who can judge that work and decide to interview, or, secondarily, to hire for contract work.

## Positioning

Confirmed role: AI Native Software Engineer. Distinguishing claim still open beyond that title. Placeholder site copy may use a provisional supporting line until the owner replaces it.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive at a website, review the owner's engineering work, and decide whether to make contact. There is no in-product workflow beyond that evaluation.

Routes: Home (`app/page.tsx`), Projects, Experience, Tech, Blog (`app/<route>/page.tsx`). Editable placeholder copy lives in `lib/content.ts`. Local development is `npm run dev`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app is Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, and TypeScript.
- Routes: Home, Projects, Experience, Tech, Blog.
- Confirmed identity: Spencer Hodson, AI Native Software Engineer.
- Projects, employers, dates, links, and contact details remain owner-supplied. Boilerplate may use clearly provisional placeholder copy until replaced.
- Open until supplied: which projects appear, which links are public, and how a visitor makes contact.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Spencer Hodson
- Role: AI Native Software Engineer
- Reference craft the owner liked: soft-dark personal portfolios in the spirit of https://sawad.framer.website/ (stage, calm sheet, identity-first). Not a license to clone that template.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner has real projects, a bio, and links. Those materials are not yet in the repository.

Future work must not present invented employers, metrics, client names, testimonials, or quotes as real. Provisional boilerplate copy is allowed when labeled as placeholder for the owner to replace.

## Product Principles

1. The full-time hiring decision comes first. Freelance is present and secondary.
2. Proof is the owner's real software engineering work. Unsupplied sections stay empty.
3. The site's job is a decision: a visitor should be able to judge fit and act.
4. Supplied materials are the only source of truth for identity and work.
